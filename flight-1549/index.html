208 Seconds

January 15, 2009, 3:25 pm. You are the captain of Flight 1549 from LaGuardia, New York, to Charlotte, with 150 passengers and 5 crew on board. A minute and a half after takeoff the airplane will fly into a flock of geese and both engines will stop. From that moment every decision is yours: who flies, what you tell the controller, where you go, and when you prepare the passengers.

Graphics:
Cockpit and radio voices with subtitles · the departure controller’s voice is the real FAA recording · headphones recommended · Sources and licenses

Before takeoff

Your seat: the left seat — the captain. Drag with the mouse to turn your head; double-click to look ahead again.
Flying by hand: after the strike, move the stick (arrow keys, WASD, the on-screen stick or a gamepad) and the airplane is yours. Pull = nose up and slower; push = faster. With no engines, every metre of height counts.
Cockpit controls: press them yourself — the APU on the overhead panel, the thrust levers and the flaps (F) on the pedestal, the DITCHING button and the evacuation. Hover over any control to see what it is. The list on the side reminds you what is needed — click an item to see where it is.
Radio and cabin: T or the VHF1 key to talk to the controller, P to speak to the passengers.
The display in front of you: the dashed green circle on the navigation display is how far you can still glide. An airport outside it = you will not reach it.
Note: a re-creation of a real accident in which everyone on board survived.
